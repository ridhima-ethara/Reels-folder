# ml-three-ways: post

## Caption

AI learns in 3 main ways. Can you tell them apart? 🧠

01 Supervised: learns from examples WITH the answers
02 Unsupervised: finds patterns with no answers at all
03 Reinforcement: trial, error, rewards

Quick quiz from the end: an app groups your photos by face, without any names. Is that 1, 2 or 3? Drop your answer in the comments 👇

#machinelearning #artificialintelligence #aiexplained #learnai #supervisedlearning #reinforcementlearning #datascience #techreels

## Quiz answer (pin as a reply after a few hours)
2, unsupervised: it groups similar faces without being told who anyone is (clustering).
