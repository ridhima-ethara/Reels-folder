Three main ways AI learns. Let's go. One: supervised learning. You show it examples with the answers. This email is spam, this one isn't. Soon it labels new emails by itself. Two: unsupervised learning. No answers at all. It finds patterns on its own, like grouping shoppers who buy alike. Three: reinforcement learning. Trial and error. A reward when it wins, a penalty when it fails. AlphaGo was trained this way. Plot twist: ChatGPT used more than one. Supervised examples first, then rewards from human rankings. Quick quiz. An app groups your photos by face, without any names. Is that one, two, or three? Comment your answer.
