## Facts and sources
- Learning from reward is what animal trainers have done for thousands of years; RL is that idea for machines (dog analogy). ACM Turing Award 2024 release: https://awards.acm.org/about/2024-turing
- AlphaGo beat Lee Sedol 4-1, Seoul, March 2016; Lee was an 18-time world champion ("Go legend"). CBC: https://www.cbc.ca/news/science/google-deepmind-go-final-1.3491693 , Trusted Reviews: https://www.trustedreviews.com/news/google-deepmind-ai-finishes-off-go-champ-in-style
- AlphaGo used reinforcement learning (taught itself by self-play after starting from human games). i-programmer on the Turing Award: https://i-programmer.info/news/239-awards-and-prizes/17879-turing-award-for-reinforcement-learning-pioneers.html
- ChatGPT was trained with Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback: trainers ranked several model answers, the rankings trained a reward model, and the model was tuned against it. OpenAI: https://openai.com/blog/chatgpt
  - Simplification on screen: "rankings became the treats" (strictly, rankings trained a reward model that gives the treats). "Partly" because ChatGPT also had supervised training first.
