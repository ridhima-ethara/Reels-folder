## Caption

Nobody gave this AI the answers. 🤖

It learned the way you'd train a dog: try something, get a treat (or not), adjust, repeat.
That's reinforcement learning. It's how AlphaGo beat Lee Sedol 4-1, and part of how ChatGPT was trained.
