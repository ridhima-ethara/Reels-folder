Nobody gave this AI the answers. It learned by trial and error. That's reinforcement learning. Think of training a dog. It sits, it gets a treat. It chews your shoes, no treat. Now make the AI the dog. It tries a move, gets a reward or a penalty, and adjusts. Then it does that again and again. That's how AlphaGo beat Go legend Lee Sedol, four games to one, back in 2016. Here's the twist. ChatGPT was trained partly the same way. Humans ranked its answers, and those rankings became the treats. Want my simple guide to reinforcement learning?
